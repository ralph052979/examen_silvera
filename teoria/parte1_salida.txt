  p       t (s)     S(p)     E(p)          Karp-Flatt e(p)
  1       100.0   1.0000   1.0000                        -
  4        30.0   3.3333   0.8333                   0.0667
  16       11.0   9.0909   0.5682                   0.0507
  24        9.0  11.1111   0.4630                   0.0504

  Con f = 0.0541, E(p) = 1/(f p + 1 - f) = 0.5 en p = (1 + f)/f = 19.5
  Con f = 0.0544, E(p) = 1/(f p + 1 - f) = 0.5 en p = (1 + f)/f = 19.4
